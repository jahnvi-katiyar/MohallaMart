# MVP Ideation

## Product Concept

**MohallaMart** is a lightweight digital storefront and local product discovery platform that connects neighborhood shopkeepers with nearby customers.

The goal is not to build a full-scale e-commerce marketplace, but to make local shop inventory **discoverable and easier to inquire about**.

---

## Target Users

### Shopkeepers
Need a simple way to:
- Showcase products
- Update availability
- Receive customer inquiries
- Communicate with customers

### Customers
Need a way to:
- Find products in nearby shops
- Check availability
- Ask questions before visiting
- Contact the shopkeeper directly

---

## Core MVP Idea

```text
Shopkeeper
    ↓
Create Shop Profile
    ↓
Add Products + Stock
    ↓
Publish Catalog
    ↓
Customer Searches
    ↓
Views Product
    ↓
Checks Availability
    ↓
Chats With Shopkeeper
    ↓
Visits / Reserves / Buys
```

---

## Key Solution

### 1. Digital Storefront
Each shop gets a simple online catalog containing:

- Shop information
- Product images
- Product names
- Prices
- Categories
- Stock status

### 2. Local Product Discovery
Customers can search for products and discover participating nearby shops.

### 3. Product-Based Chat
Customers can start a conversation directly on a specific product.

Example:

> **Blue Cotton Shirt — ₹699**  
> Customer: "Do you have XL available?"

The shopkeeper receives the inquiry with the product context.

### 4. Inquiry Management
Shopkeepers can view, respond to, and resolve customer inquiries from one dashboard.

---

## USP

### **"Discover Locally. Chat Directly. Buy Nearby."**

MohallaMart acts as a digital bridge between **local shop inventory and local customer demand**.

It helps customers discover products without unnecessary shop visits while allowing shopkeepers to go digital without managing a complex e-commerce system.

---

## Optional WhatsApp Integration

The platform can provide:

**[Chat on MohallaMart] [Continue on WhatsApp]**

WhatsApp is an optional communication channel. The core platform remains responsible for product discovery, catalog management, and inquiry tracking.
